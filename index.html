    Transcribe a recording

    Turns a finished recording (or an imported Voice Memo) into a timestamped transcript. The model downloads once per device and is kept, so after that it works offline. It takes a while: expect several minutes per hour of audio on a laptop, and longer on a phone. Keep Recall open until it finishes.

    On a laptop you can also queue a whole subject: select its folder on the home screen and tap Transcribe all. Leave Recall open and the laptop plugged in, and it works through them one by one (fine overnight).

    Timetable

    From 20 minutes before a class until 15 minutes after, New recording is named like “Systems Engineering · Lecture 6” and filed in that subject's folder. Imported Voice Memos recorded during a class are filed the same way. If you stop and start again after a break, it becomes Part 2 of the same lecture. Tip: tap Pause during breaks to keep it all in one recording.

    Google Drive sync

    How to get a Client ID (free, ~10 minutes, once)
    1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and create a project (e.g. “Recall”).
    2. Open APIs & Services → Library, search Google Drive API, click Enable.
    3. Open Google Auth Platform (OAuth consent screen): choose External, fill in app name and your email. Under Audience, add your own Gmail as a test user.
    4. Go to Clients → Create client → type Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins add:
    5. Copy the Client ID and paste it above, then tap Connect & sync now.

    Recall only asks for access to files it creates itself (a folder called “Recall Notes” in your Drive). It can't see your other files.
